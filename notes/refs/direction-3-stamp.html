Direction 3 — Entry Stamp

Type
Superclarendon for names, dishes and stamp faces — a slab with enough weight to read as pressed ink. Avenir Next for body. SF Mono for labels and coordinates.
Signature
Every visit is an entry stamp, one ink per show, rotated slightly and overlapping. A place he went to three times carries three stamps, and you see that before reading anything. Across the site the four inks are the only colour system there is.
States
Closed places keep the ring and lose the ink, struck through like a cancelled stamp — present, visibly spent. Kind changes the dot's corner radius where known. Generic pins are stamped like any other visit; a meal with people whose names nobody wrote down still happened.
Risk
Nearest of the three to the "Bourdain homage" default CLAUDE.md warns about. It stays clear by being a document rather than a magazine: cool grey stock instead of warm cream, a slab instead of a high-contrast display serif, and four desaturated inks instead of a terracotta accent.
